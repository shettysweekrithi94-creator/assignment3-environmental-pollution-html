Environmental Pollution

Environmental pollution is one of the most serious global issues affecting human health, wildlife, and climate. Pollution occurs due to harmful substances released into air, water, and land, leading to long-term damage to ecosystems.

The images used on this page highlight major pollution sources and encourage people to understand the impact and take responsibility for protecting the environment.

Content Description

Environmental pollution is a major real-world problem that affects air, water, and land. It is mainly caused by industrial emissions, vehicle smoke, plastic waste, and improper disposal of garbage. High pollution levels lead to serious health issues and damage to natural ecosystems.

The main image used on this page is a pollution map of India, which shows different pollution levels across regions. The image map highlights areas with high, moderate, and lower pollution using interactive regions.

The responsive images displayed using the <picture> element change based on screen size to provide a better viewing experience on mobile and desktop devices. These images help in visually explaining the impact of pollution and the importance of environmental protection.
